CYBERPLAYGROUND / NETWORK TOOLKIT

Know your network.

Public IP discovery, public-IP ownership information and a browser-based speed test. Requests run only when you click a button. Results are not stored by this site.

🌐 What's my IP?

Shows the public address visible to an external service, not necessarily your device's local address.

Waiting for request.Uses api.ipify.org. VPNs and proxies may change the result.

🔎 Who owns this IP?

Waiting for address.Uses ipwho.is for approximate country, ISP and ASN. Location is not a person's physical address.

⚡ Internet speed test

Measures approximate latency, download and upload throughput to Cloudflare's network. Uses up to about 5 MB per run. Results vary by Wi-Fi, VPN, browser and server.

Not started.Cloudflare receives your IP and test traffic. The test transfers data, so avoid metered connections.

Privacy: Your browser contacts the named providers directly. These providers may process your IP under their own policies. Do not use this page to investigate private people or infer precise locations.
